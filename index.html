About Me

✦ · ✦ · ✦

Welcome, wanderer. This is a place for my writings that need to breathe, thoughts on books and films that I love, the passion projects I code and my attempt to share wisdom as I learn about different topics.

My mother says I have a gypsy soul, and I cannot deny it -- I love traveling; I am completely consumed by wanderlust.

If you are looking for a more professional introduction, then hey there. I am a junior at Colby College in Maine, USA. I am originally from Bangladesh. When I think of my career, I want to dedicate myself to biotechnology research, and that's why I'm double majoring in Biology with a concentration in Neuroscience and Computer Science with a concentration in Artificial Intelligence.

As you can see, the theme of my website is different shades of blue. Not because blue is my favorite color, but because I have lived all my life at the plinth of blues -- in the sense of melancholy and hope.

So, pull up a chair by the lantern light and explore my world!
